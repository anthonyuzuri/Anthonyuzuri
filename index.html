Before you ask

Questions you may have.

I’ve been burned by lead agencies. Why is this different?

Let me guess. Fifty leads a month, mostly tire kickers, and nobody remembered agreeing to a call. Lead agencies are volume players. We don’t sell leads. We’d rather get you five real conversations with companies hiring right now than fifty maybes. And if we introduce you to garbage, we lose credibility with you and with the company. So we only make intros that make sense for both sides.

I already do outreach. What do you add?

If you’re already getting qualified intros every month, you probably don’t need us. If it’s hit or miss, three things. Timing: we work off companies that just opened roles in your lane, not basic filters like industry and title. Framing: the company hears “I know a specialist who places exactly this,” not another recruiter pitching. Time: we run the front end, so you spend your hours closing and placing.

Who are the companies?

That depends on you. Your list gets built after we agree on your box: the roles you fill best, the company sizes you place well with, the industries that pay you. Say you place controls engineers. We’d look for manufacturers that posted controls or automation roles in the last few weeks, especially ones hiring for several at once. Building that list is real work, so it starts when we work together.

What counts as a qualified intro?

A decision maker at a company inside your agreed box, who has confirmed an active hiring need or is open to a new agency, with a meeting booked. Anything less doesn’t count toward your number. Intros that miss the standard get replaced at no cost.
