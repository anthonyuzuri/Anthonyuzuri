05 · Before you ask

Questions you may have.

What does an engagement actually look like?

Three phases: a survey that maps and prices your operational leaks, a fixed-scope build measured in weeks, and an ongoing evolution phase where the live system is monitored, hardened, and extended. You see the map before you commit to the machine.

Will this replace my team?

It replaces their busywork, not their judgment. The system drafts, routes, chases, and reconciles; your people decide, negotiate, and build relationships. Clients typically redeploy hours into selling · not headcount out the door.

We already have automations. Do we start over?

Never. The survey audits what exists first · what’s live, what’s broken, what’s redundant. Anything that works gets kept and wired in. You pay for the gaps, not for re-plumbing what already flows.

Which stacks do you build on?

HubSpot, Salesforce, GoHighLevel, and Zoho on the CRM side; Stripe, Slack, ClickUp, Make, Twilio, Instantly, and Calendly around them; Claude when the machine needs to read, write, or reason. If it has an API, it can join the circuit.

What happens when something breaks at 2 a.m.?

The system notices before you do. Every workflow logs its runs, exceptions page us, not you, and failures queue safely instead of silently dropping. Evolution means the 2 a.m. problem is our problem.

The friction isn’t free. It bills you nightly.
